## v3.1 Refactor (efficiency + mechanism strengthening)

- **Dual 1-D HGF plasticity gate** (`O(d²) → O(d)`): removed `fisher_mask` /
  `fisher_mask_inv`; output-side gate reuses HGF `running_var`, input-side reuses `C_x`
  diagonal; row/column 1-D scaling, structurally identical to Hi-DoRA.
- **GHL** global-direction Hebbian: update sign = convex combo of local reward and global
  gradient/loss direction.
- **SPHeRe** low-dim structural projection: orthogonal penalty upgraded to
  `W₀-overlap + low-rank-covariance structure` loss.
- **STDP** noisy gradient: volatility/conflict/state-dependent Gaussian noise in `_flush_hebbian`.
- **BCM** adaptive threshold, **WTA** hard competition (top-k of r), **DLAM prototype
  memory** extracted during sleep.

## Benchmark Suite

Evaluated on **Qwen2.5-0.5B**, concept-drift protocol SST-2 → MRPC → QNLI → RTE → CoLA,
never revisited.

### ex1 — Concept-drift forgetting (3 methods × 5 tasks × 250 steps)

| Method | ACC ↑ | BWT ↑ | Forgetting ↓ |
| :--- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `lora` (peft) | 0.5976 | **−0.0471** | **0.0538** |
| `aeloru-safe` | **0.6020** | **−0.0017** | **0.0211** |
| `aeloru-default` | 0.5311 | −0.0133 | 0.0273 |

**Forgetting reduced 60.8%**, BWT improved 96.4% (near zero forgetting), ACC slightly higher.

### ex2 — Subspace decoupling (6 ablations)

LoRA `corr_abs` is 2.8× AeloRu's, TC 4.6× higher, effective rank 28% lower, forgetting
3.8× higher. `nowta` / `nosphere` contribute most to decoupling.
⚠️ Counter-intuitive: `aeloru-nopem` (PEM off) has *lower* `corr_abs` — PEM is a
**negative contributor** to r-space decorrelation (see ex3).

### ex3 — Redundant vs. Functional coupling (the PEM question)

Decomposes correlation into functional (`corr_useful`) vs. redundant (`corr_useless` /
`coup_red`) parts via Gaussian closed-form task information + Fisher weighting.

- **PEM itself has no task selectivity** (open-loop SEL ≈ 0).
- Selectivity is a **closed-loop emergent effect**: PEM damages correlations → BP repairs
  only the task-useful ones.
- Selection is **energy-conditioned / spectral**: when redundant coupling dominates the
  principal direction, PEM's precision-weighted decorrelation clips functional correlations.
- Conclusion: paper §5.6 wording must be revised (PEM not deleted); PEM is a
  precision-weighted, indiscriminate decorrelation pressure in low-rank space.

### ex4 — From-scratch PEM space

`pem_space` ablation: output-space PEM gives +2.74pp accuracy from scratch; low-rank-space
PEM gives −1.40pp — but **both** raise coupling and lower effective rank, so PEM's benefit
comes from fit modulation, not decoupling.

## 13 Blocking Defects Documented (porting cognitive modules to Transformers)

Top findings (full list in `md/ex1.md`, `md/fix1.md`):

1. `run_predictive_neural_dynamics` only supported 2D; Transformer hidden states are 3D → crash.
2. Gradient fully truncated (second call under `no_grad`); fixed with straight-through residual.
3. **Predictive-coding neural dynamics corrupt representations** (`ndi>0` → model can't learn).
4. **Homeostatic gain dimensionality error** (`1/var` vs `1/√var`).
5. `clamp(0,1)` domain constraint truncates ~half of negative activations.
6. `spectral_filter` leftover debug `print`s.
7. `spectral_filter` leftover debug `print`s.
8. V2 `experiment_1.py` contrast-unfair (base not frozen).
9. `ΔW` precision trap (bf16 subtraction → fake rank).
10. Heatmap under-estimated correlation (sign cancellation).
11. `_append_hgf_delta` never called → HongWen conflict detection dead.
12. `batch_forward_lora` double-scaling hazard.
13. `named_buffers` dotted-path `setattr` bug → sub-module buffers not moved to device.

Of these, "full-output-space predictive coding corrupts pretrained representations" and
"homeostatic gain dimensionality error" are flagged as **generally cautionary** for similar
neuro-inspired work.
